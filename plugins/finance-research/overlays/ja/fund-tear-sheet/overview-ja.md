# Japanese overview tab (概要)

The same content as the English Overview tab (`overview.md`), in natural Japanese for a reader whose first language is Japanese but who has lived and worked in the US for many years and isn't familiar with investing in either country.
- **Write natural Japanese from the finished English overview,** keeping tense and dating exactly: a dated past reading must not become a present estimate.
- **Numbers and dates stay exactly as on the English tabs** (write "Oct 27", not 10月27日) ($400.2B, 0.20%, 4.02%, Sep 30, 1y/5y/10y). Write "~$3.5B" as 約$3.5B (drop the tilde, never both).
- **Financial terms:** plain Japanese first, then the English term in parentheses on first use — 経費率（expense ratio）, 純資産総額（total net assets）, 7日間SEC利回り（7-day SEC yield）, 指数（index）, 目標年（target date）. Don't introduce Japan-market fund vocabulary (信託報酬, 基準価額 conventions) as if familiar, and don't compare with Japanese funds.
- Fund, issuer and index names stay in their original form (Invesco QQQ Trust, Nasdaq-100).
- です・ます体, short sentences.
- Section labels: 概要, 中身は何か, コストはいくらか, これまでの成績, 値動きの大きさ, 持つ前に知っておきたいこと, 読み方, 英語版での場所.
- End with: この概要は英語版タブ（Tear sheet / Detail / Glossary）に基づいています。投資助言ではありません。

**Length:** about 1,800–2,800 Japanese characters.

The checks in `overview.md` ("Simplify without drifting") apply to the Japanese text too.

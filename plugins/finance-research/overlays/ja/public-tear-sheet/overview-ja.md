# Japanese overview tab (概要)

The same content as the English Overview tab (`overview.md`), written for a reader whose first language is Japanese but who has lived and worked in the US for many years and isn't familiar with investing in either country.

- **Write natural Japanese from the finished English overview,** keeping tense and dating exactly: a dated past reading (…と見ていました, Sep 10 時点) must not become a present estimate (…と見ています). It's a rewrite for the reader, not a line-by-line translation. Keep the structure (summary + five questions, each with 読み方 / How to read and 英語版での場所 / where on the tear sheet).
- **Numbers and dates stay exactly as on the English tabs: $38.0B, 18%, 48×, Nov 19, Apr–Jun.** Write "~$3.5B" as 約$3.5B (drop the tilde, never both). Don't convert to 億 or 兆, and don't convert currency. The reader matches numbers between the tabs, and conversion is where errors creep in.
- **Financial terms:** plain Japanese wording first, then the English term in parentheses on first use, e.g. 時価総額（market cap）, 株価収益率（P/E）, 売上高（revenue）. The English term is what the reader will see on the tear sheet and in the Glossary, so it's the link between the tabs. Don't introduce Japanese-market shorthand (PER, PBR, 決算短信 conventions) as if it were familiar, and don't compare with how things work in Japan.
- **Company, product and person names** stay in their original form (Take-Two, Grand Theft Auto VI). Add katakana only if it genuinely helps.
- Use polite plain style (です・ます). Keep sentences short.
- Section labels: 概要, 何をしている会社か, 成長しているか, 利益は出ているか, 企業価値と割高・割安, 今後の注目点とリスク, 読み方, 英語版での場所. For private companies, replace the middle three with: どのくらいの規模か・その根拠は, 投資家はいくらの価値をつけたか, 上場企業にとってなぜ重要か.
- End with one line: この概要は英語版タブ（Tear sheet / Detail / Glossary）に基づいています。投資助言ではありません。
- Two similar measures keep distinct names in Japanese too (ネットブッキングの会社予想 vs 売上高の会社予想).

**Length:** about 2,000–3,000 Japanese characters.

The checks in `overview.md` ("Simplify without drifting") apply to the Japanese text too.

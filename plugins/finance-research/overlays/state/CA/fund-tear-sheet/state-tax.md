# State tax: California

- **US government income:** California exempts a fund's dividends attributable to US government obligations only if the fund held at least 50% of its assets in such obligations at the end of each quarter of its tax year; if it fails the test, none of it is exempt. Issuers publish which funds met the threshold; the large issuers' tax centers list them each January. Report whether this fund met it for the latest tax year, with the source.
- **Accounts:** in a traditional IRA or 401(k), withdrawals are fully taxable by California whatever the fund earned; qualified Roth withdrawals aren't taxed.
- **Rate bands:** run fund_calc with one `tax,state_rate` row for each of 4, 6, 9.3, 11.3 and 13.3 (illustrative California marginal rates; 0 is always shown). Check the current schedule on the Franchise Tax Board site if the rates may have changed.
